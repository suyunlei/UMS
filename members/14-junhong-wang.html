Junhong Wang

王俊鸿

Visiting Researcher

Junhong is currently a visiting researcher at HKUST(GZ). He has conducted research on thermal environments and shade. At HKUST(GZ), he is studying commuters’ experienced walkability in the first and last mile. His research focuses on human-environment interaction, with the aim of building more sustainable, smarter, and more livable cities.

Research interests

  • Urban climate
  • Urban morphology
  • Human mobility

Education

  • Master of Urban Planning, National University of Singapore 2025
  • Bachelor of Urban Planning and Design, Xi'an Jiaotong Liverpool University/University of Liverpool 2023
