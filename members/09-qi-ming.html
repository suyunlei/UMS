Qiming Liu is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Urban and Rural Planning at Renmin University of China and serves as a research assistant in the UMS team at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His research primarily focuses on employing a variety of methods, including GIS, GeoAI, and spatial statistics, to systematically investigate urban space quality evaluation and improvement, healthy city planning, and spatiotemporal behavior analysis, all within the context of urban studies using multi-source urban big data.
