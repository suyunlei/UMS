Koichi and I will be hosting a webinar on Decarbonising the City at 8 PM - 10 PM (SG/Hong Kong) today. The webinar is part of the International Forum of Urbanism (IFoU) series established by Prof Zhang Ye and Dr. Vivienne Wang to promote exchange and collaboration among early career urban researchers. Our talk will focus on transportation (Koichi), buildings (myself), and post-carbon cities (Hashem, PhD candidate at Politecnico di Torino.Aside from our talks, the talk will feature a 30 minutes panel discussion with Professor Jin Yeu Tsou (Raymond Hu Professor of Architecture, CUHK) and Professor Arno Schlueter (Professor of Architecture and Building Systems, ETH) working on energy and buildings.
