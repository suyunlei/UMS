Hanzhi was an MPhil student in the UGOD Thrust at HKUST(GZ). With a background in sociology, his research explores the relationship between urban form and urban society within the broader interaction between space and society. He combines sociology and urban morphology to study sustainable urban development. His interests also include computational sociology and the application of new technological methods to sociological studies.
