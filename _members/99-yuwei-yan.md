Yuwei Yan is a PhD student in the Computational Media and Arts Thrust, Information Hub, at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His research interests include generative models, agent systems, and simulation. His work explores LLM-driven agents for modeling human behavior and social interactions, including contributions to AgentSociety and the AgentSociety Challenge.
