Baichuan is currently an MPhil student in the UGOD Thrust at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). He is interested in understanding how people perceive and interact with the urban built environment, and how computational methods can support more human-centered urban analysis and design. At HKUST(GZ), Baichuan hopes to integrate human perception, urban data, and computational methods to better understand the built environment and foster more human-centered approaches to sustainable urban development.
